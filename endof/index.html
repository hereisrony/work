The end of foreign

A feature essay moving through migration, cinema, archives, classrooms, interfaces and the small humiliations of being translated.

Historical assimilation meets synthetic media.

Personal memory meets the machinery built to make everybody legible.

A child who learned languages to reach other worlds meets an adult living at the precise moment when those worlds can be brought to her already translated.
