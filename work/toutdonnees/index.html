TOUT DONNÉES

interdisciplinary journey, 2017

IGLOÙ’s second annual event invited visitors on a weekend-long digital and multisensory journey at DOC, a former high school turned artist residency in Paris. Interdisciplinary artists created immersive experiences that explored how new technologies shape daily life, using interactive video, sound installations, and new media to let visitors “listen to drawings” and “watch music.”

This event used digital systems to mediate human interaction and sensory experiences, blending the physical and digital realms to develop new methods of interdisciplinary artistic production.

Curator: Rony Efrat
Administrative Director: Alexandra Beraldin
IGLOÙ Members: Elena Sukhanakova, Nicolas Lefebvre, Antoine Hirel, Mélie Néel
